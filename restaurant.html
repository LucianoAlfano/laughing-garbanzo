Common questions

Before a customer reaches out.

Good pages answer practical questions without making the visitor call for every detail.

Can the menu be updated without a developer?

Yes. A real version can use a simple managed source so staff can change dishes, prices, and availability.

Can it connect to our reservation platform?

The build can link or integrate with the booking tool the restaurant already uses, subject to that tool’s capabilities.

Can private dining be handled separately?

Yes. Event inquiries can collect date, guest count, budget range, and dietary needs before staff follows up.
