WHAT CHANGED

1. Both of you, everywhere
   Visible copy, button labels and the homepage structured data now name
   Anthony and Luciano. "1 — Person, from first message to launch" became
   "2 — Brothers". A founders panel was added to the homepage and gallery.
   Your existing anthonyalfano157@gmail.com address is unchanged; no new
   mailbox was invented.

2. Background animations rebuilt
   Each theme now has its own scene instead of generic drifting particles:
   candlelight and embers, a wind field with fluttering leaves, carbon weave
   and specular sweeps, oven glow with steam ribbons, speed streaks with a
   heartbeat shockwave, silk ribbons and petals, a self-drawing blueprint, a
   growing ledger chart, and an aurora constellation that leans toward the
   cursor. Depth parallax responds to pointer and scroll. Glows are
   pre-rendered sprites rather than a new gradient per particle, and frame
   times are sampled so density drops on slower devices and recovers when
   there is headroom. Your fork cursor and its dark shadow are untouched.

3. Navigation repaired
   Restaurant subpages pointed every footer and "Themes" link back to
   restaurant.html. They now reach professional.html, landscape.html,
   themes.html and index.html correctly. Skip links target #main. The active
   page is marked in the tabs; single-page demos highlight the section in
   view.

4. Live menu and wine
   Both loaders now validate headers, handle quoted and multi-line CSV
   fields, time out after 15 seconds, refresh every 10 minutes while the tab
   is visible, and re-check on return. A first-load failure says so plainly
   instead of showing invented bottles; a failed refresh keeps the last good
   data with a warning. The restaurant homepage now reads the same published
   sheet as the menu page rather than a separate hard-coded list.

5. Honest forms
   The reservation form previously tried to email a fictional
   .example address. It now validates required fields, rejects past dates and
   previews the request, clearly labelled as a demonstration. The agency
   inquiry form gained a message preview, a copy fallback and a clear-draft
   button; drafts are session-scoped and the status no longer implies an
   email was sent.

6. Guided theme finder
   find-your-theme.html asks for business type, priority and visual style,
   reorders all eight demos with the reasons shown, and supports search,
   shortlist-only view, quick-view dialogs, up to three shortlisted
   directions, a preferred pick, undo, shareable links, and a project brief
   you can copy, download, print or open as an email draft. Notes are never
   put into a share link. The gallery links to it in three places.

7. Smaller fixes
   Stored colour modes are validated before use. Gallery filters preserve
   other URL parameters. Anchor jumps move keyboard focus. The mobile drawer
   is inert while closed and scrolls when long. Reveal animations no longer
   hide content when JavaScript is unavailable. The header collapses at
   1100px instead of overlapping. robots.txt no longer blocks crawlers from
   reading the demos' own noindex tags.
